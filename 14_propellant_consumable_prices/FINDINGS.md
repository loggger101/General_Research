## R101 - LH2 production-cost anchors + hydrazine citation negative finding (2026-10-02; +2 sources T2x2, both hosted; 1 extraction CSV)

All three upstream repos were re-swept at their current HEADs first and carried no new institutional claims (economicspace@29a0309 already recon'd R100 - internal model docs only; AsteroidCatalog @6a2cfbc = #17/#18 docs, citations already registered), so this round continued down d14's queue: the LH2 rows were the next cells without an institutional production-cost anchor (the IEA HGR 2024 row from R100 is context-only - its cost figures sit in vector charts).

- `nasa_cr_73226_study_cost_system_analysis_lh2_production` [T2, full_text_hosted] - NASA CR 73226 (June 1968), N. C. Hallett of Air Products & Chemicals for the Mission Analysis Division at NASA Ames Research Center; NTRS record 19680018755, determination GOV_PUBLIC_USE_PERMITTED. Abstract verbatim: 'Composite system analyses based on preliminary NASA hypersonic transport (HST) liquid hydrogen requirements indicate estimated average product cost of 7.7 to 8.8 cents per pound.' Table 5-2 unit costs by process (Bangkok stand-alone case): steam reforming 11.8 / partial oxidation 11.6 / coal gasification 19.9 / electrolysis 18.4 cents/lb LH2, with the report concluding 'the liquid hydrogen facilities employing electrolysis or coal gasification sub-systems are not economically competitive'. The storage-analysis section prices boil-off losses at an assumed 'value of liquid hydrogen of 10 cents/lb' - a loss-accounting value, NOT a delivered price.
- `nasa_cr_159163_economics_hydrogen_production_liquefaction_updated_1980` [T2, full_text_hosted] - NASA CR 159163 (November 1979), C. R. Baker of the Linde Division / Union Carbide for NASA Langley Research Center; NTRS record 19800002991, determination GOV_PUBLIC_USE_PERMITTED. Updates three predecessor contractor reports to mid-1980 dollars: 'the unit value for hydrogen is calculated to be as follows' - Liquid H2 Product $1.10/kg ($0.499/lb), Gaseous H2 Feedstock $0.586/kg ($0.266/lb) at 3¢/kWh electricity + fuel gas $2.84/GJ; the heavy-water section separately evaluates 'Liquid hydrogen ... at a fixed price of $0.50 per lb for the loss of product item' - an internal cross-check confirming both figures are mid-1980s dollars.

**Escalation to 2024 dollars** (World Bank WDI FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG annual chain, already registered in domain 23 as `world_bank_usa_annual_cpi_inflation` - no new source needed): 1968..2024 x9.018 -> CR-73226's average product cost = $1.53-$1.75/kg (pre-handling production); mid-1980s base 1980/1984..2024 x3.807/x3.020 -> CR-159163's LH2 product unit value = $3.32-$4.19/kg. Upstream's hydrolox note says 'LH2 base price $3-6/kg (NASA contracts); bulk-handling overhead lifts effective cost to ~$10/kg' - the escalated institutional figure sits INSIDE the stated band, so this is a consistency anchor: no re-pin proposed and no rc opened. The '~$10/kg' with handling overhead remains above what either report prices (both are plant-gate production costs), which is exactly what the note's own wording says.

**Documented negative finding - hydrazine commercial price**: spacecost's Hydrazine row cites 'commercial $75.8/kg (AIAA 2024)' but no such paper resolves: a Crossref sweep of AIAA Joint Propulsion proceedings (hydrazine + cost, all years and 2023-2025) plus NTRS searches ('hydrazine propellant cost', 'cost analysis liquid hydrogen') found NO indexed cost-of-hydrazine paper - the closest hits are low-cost thruster/catalyst papers that do not price hydrazine per kg. The row's only institutional anchor stays DLA FY20 ($30.5/kg, still HTTP 403 from this machine); until a specific DOI for the $75.8 figure is identified it remains an unregistered upstream citation - recorded here so future rounds do not re-search blindly.
